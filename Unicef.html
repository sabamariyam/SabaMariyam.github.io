Unicef Research

Project Overview

The Girl’s Education & Skills Partnership research project explored how the Passport 2 Earning (P2E) platform could better engage female learners and encourage them to continue their learning journey.

The research used qualitative methods to understand learners’ experiences with course content, its relevance and quality, and the factors influencing their motivation and engagement. Learner perspectives were gathered across Hyderabad, Pune, and Delhi and used to develop recommendations for improving the learning experience.

My Role - Young Female Researcher

Research Focus

The research focused on understanding:

“From a learner’s perspective, how can P2E course content be made more engaging for girls to continue their learning journey?”
We explored how content quality, relevance, presentation, interactive elements, motivation, and representation influenced learners’ experiences and willingness to continue learning.
The research also examined similarities and differences in learner perspectives across the three research locations.

Research Methodology

The project followed a qualitative research approach designed to capture detailed learner experiences and perspectives.

Conducted field research with female P2E learners
Conducted and contributed to Focus Group Discussions (FGDs)
Gathered qualitative perspectives on course content and learner experience
Analyzed recurring themes and patterns in participant responses
Compared findings across Hyderabad, Pune, and Delhi
Synthesized qualitative insights into actionable recommendations

Research Locations

Hyderabad · Pune · Delhi

The research brought together perspectives from learners across different backgrounds and locations, allowing the team to identify both common themes and location-specific insights.

Analysis & Findings

Advanced & Relevant Content

Learners expressed interest in more advanced and in-depth content, particularly in technical subjects, to support continued learning and progression beyond beginner-level courses.

Interactive Learning

Participants highlighted the value of quizzes, gamified challenges, collaborative activities, hands-on projects, short videos, animations, and real-world problem solving as ways to make learning more engaging.

Motivation & Recognition

Learners suggested expanding recognition beyond certificates through badges, leaderboards, mentorship, internships, job referrals, and other incentives that could encourage continued participation.

Female Representation

The research highlighted the importance of female role models and representation in technology and leadership, with participants indicating that diverse career pathways could make the content more relevant and motivating.

User Experience

Some learners identified opportunities to improve platform navigation, accessibility, and the mobile experience, particularly around accessing course progress and moving between courses.

Recommendations

Introduce more advanced technical courses and deeper learning modules.
Incorporate more real-world examples, demonstrations, and practical applications.
Expand gamified and collaborative learning experiences such as challenges, team activities, and interactive forums.
Introduce additional forms of recognition including badges, leaderboards, mentorship, networking, and career opportunities.
Continue using short videos and animations while incorporating real-life scenarios to improve relatability.
Improve platform navigation and mobile usability.
Increase the representation of women in technology and leadership through role models, success stories, and diverse career pathways.
